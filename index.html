What To Start Today

How To Track BP

  1. Sit quietly for 5 minutes.
  2. Feet flat, back supported, arm supported at heart level.
  3. No talking during the reading.
  4. Take 2 readings, 1 minute apart.
  5. Log both readings, plus pulse.
  6. Also log caffeine, medication timing, sleep, water, exercise, and salty meals.
Best times: morning before medication/caffeine, 2-3 hours after medication, and evening after it wears down.

Bottom Line

If he starts today, May 6, 2026, he could see some improvement within days to 2 weeks. A healthy or near-healthy BP range is more realistically a 1-3 month goal, meaning the main improvement window is roughly June 6-August 6, 2026.

Important: judge the average trend, not one reading. One number can be affected by stress, caffeine, recent activity, poor sleep, medication timing, hydration, and measurement technique.
Do not ignore urgent symptoms: chest pain, fainting, severe shortness of breath, one-sided weakness or numbness, vision changes, confusion, severe sudden headache, or a reading near 180/120 should be treated as urgent.
